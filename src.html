Case A · New York sourcing

Turning public data into a commercial advantage.

We needed to grow our New York supply. But finding reliable transport companies meant manually searching multiple public databases, checking licences and hunting for contact details.

The information was there. Nobody had connected it. So I did.

I combined six NYC public datasets into one sourcing intelligence platform.

What it delivers

Know who's compliant.

02 · Discover

Find the way in.

Match licensed transport bases with contact details, websites and booking channels.

03 · Prioritise

Best opportunities first.

Rank companies by fleet size, upcoming licence renewals and service type. Identify the best commercial opportunities first.

Case B · Personal project · Market intelligence

Making invisible demand visible.

I'm entrepreneurial by nature, and I always start with data combined with commercial gut feeling.

I wanted competitive intelligence on the whole wellness market, to decide whether to invest in the space or open something myself. That meant understanding how wellness businesses actually fill up.

Which slots sell out? When do customers book? How far ahead do they plan? What impact does weather have on demand?

Booking availability is public. Actual booking behaviour isn't.

So I built a system that monitors public booking pages throughout the day and detects when availability disappears. Each change becomes a booking record, creating a dataset that reveals real demand patterns.

What it delivers
01 · Collect

Every slot, all day.

Automated scrapers monitor 20+ locations, checking available time slots up to 96 times a day.

02 · Understand

Every gap is a booking.

Every disappearing slot becomes a booking with a timestamp and estimated booking lead time.

03 · Predict & optimise

Patterns you can act on.

Track peak hours, sell-out rates, booking patterns, seasonality and weather correlations. AI generates weekly market insights.
